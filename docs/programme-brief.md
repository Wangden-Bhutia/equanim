### Programme design (full; implement engine for all, content for Week 0-1 now, data-driven src/data/programme.ts)
Week 0 (Day 0 ~10min): welcome, values step: pick 3 of ~12 (honesty, calm, courage, kindness, growth, reliability, curiosity, fairness, humility, freedom, connection, health) + one sentence each "What would living this look like on an ordinary day?"; baseline 8-10 self-ratings 1-5 across domains + 2 free-text situation questions; UI says not a validated instrument.
Weeks 1-4 one domain each in order: Personalisation, Inquiry, Speech, Effort. 6 days: D1 Understand (2-min lesson, concrete example, not-this caveat); D2 Recognise (spot-the-pattern, 3 short scenarios); D3 Rehearse (script/phrase to say aloud or draft, low-stakes practice); D4 Apply (real-life micro-experiment, user writes if-then "If X happens at Y, I will Z"); D5 Retrieve & extend (3 retrieval Qs this week + 1 from earlier weeks, then harder scenario); D6 Weekly review (what worked, what got in the way, "what I noticed about myself", 1-5 domain self-rating). D7 rest.
Week 5 Integration: mixed multi-skill scenarios, "hardest situation" plan, re-read values. Week 6 Consolidate: repeat baseline, side-by-side baseline vs now (own ratings, no improvement score), "evidence of change" from journal/review notes, letter to future self, maintenance plan.
Sessions unlock in order, can be done late, no penalty/streak shaming; behind => "pick up where you left off".
Content totals (eventually): ~24 lessons/sessions 1-3 min, 12 phrase banks, 16 retrieval Qs per domain easy/med/hard, 12 integration scenarios, weekly review prompts. Reuse existing scenarios/practices where fitting.
Spaced retrieval: earlier Qs resurface days 2, 7, 21 after first seen; max 2/day in small "Quick recall" card.
### Habit loop
If-then plan builder "When ___, I will ___" with per-domain situation/response chips, one-tap suggested plan, editable. Evening check opens with morning plan: "Did the moment come up? What did you do?" 3 answers (didn't come up / tried it / didn't manage it) + optional note; "didn't manage" gets warm practical response. Optional daily reminder via downloadable recurring .ics at chosen time (no push/servers). Backup nudge every 7 days of use; Settings shows last export date.
### Today
One primary card "Today's session" (title, time estimate, one big button). Compact 3-step day strip Morning plan -> Session -> Evening check with done states. Everything else under collapsed "More for today" (quick recall, scenario of day, free journal, extra practices). Header chip "Week 2 · Day 3 of 42" + thin progress bar. Every practice offers "Think it through" vs "Write it down" (typing never required). Max 2 taps from open to session start.
